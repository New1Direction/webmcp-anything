The outreach engine, in plain English

We already graded thousands of MCP servers. This turns those grades into paying customers, almost entirely on autopilot. You only ever touch the hot replies, and even those come pre-written.

1

The grade graph already done

We have independently graded 6,771 MCP servers A to F. 2,580 of them scored D or F. Every grade sits on its own public report page. That is the raw material.

↓
2

Generate the campaign automatic

One call pulls the right targets and writes a personalized email for each, using its real failing finding (for example: "your authorize_payment tool is flagged for a secret-exfiltration surface"). No writing per lead. Out comes a spreadsheet, ready to send.

↓
3

Find the emails set up once

A finder tool (Apollo or Hunter) fills in each contact email from the company's domain. Connect it once and it runs on every batch.

↓
4

Send them set up once

A cold-email tool (Smartlead or Instantly) on a dedicated warmed domain sends the emails, spaced out, each with an unsubscribe link. You import the spreadsheet and it drips them automatically.

↓
5

Triage every reply automatic

Each reply hits our webhook and gets sorted instantly:

  • Unsubscribe: removed and never contacted again.
  • Out of office, bounce, or "not interested": logged quietly. You never see it.
  • Interested, asks price, or any real question: flagged HOT.
↓
6

Close the hot ones your only step

Each hot reply pings your Slack or Discord with the message and a ready-to-send reply already written for you. Read it, tweak if you feel like it, send. That is your entire job.

↓
7

They pay on their report page revenue

Hot leads buy the Deep Audit (one time, the fix) plus Monitoring (monthly, keeps it clean and proves it to their users). A-graders buy the Verified badge instead.

Two lists, same engine

Audit list (F-graders)
"Your server failed security. Here is exactly what to fix." Sells the Deep Audit + Monitoring.
Verified list (A-graders)
"You passed. Make it provable with a live badge." Sells Verified + Monitoring. Easy yes.

What you actually touch

  • Once: connect a sending domain + an email finder, set two secrets (alert webhook + admin token), paste one webhook URL into the email tool.
  • Forever after: read the hot pings and send the reply that is already drafted for you.

The one rule

The grades are honest and free whether or not anyone pays. This is independent audit, not a shakedown. That honesty is the entire moat, so it never gets bent to make a sale.
